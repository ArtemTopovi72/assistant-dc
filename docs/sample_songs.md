# Sample songs

Two hand-authored examples of the `{"lyrics": str, "style": str}` shape that
`music.build_structured_caption(ctx, topic, lang)` should produce, for manual
testing against a real ComfyUI once the MiniMax Music 3 weights and node
package are confirmed in place. Neither was generated by calling the LLM or
ComfyUI — they are fixtures, written directly, in the exact format the
function's docstring and prompt specify.

Theme for both: upbeat, about creativity and building things — a fit for
this project.

```
Global Metadata: Genre is upbeat indie-pop with a light electronic pulse,

Emotional arc moves from curious and tentative in verse one, through

timbre, conversational and close-mic'd in the verses (almost spoken-sung),

harmonies stack under the final chorus and outro; no vocoder or heavy

Arrangement: Verse one is sparse — a single clean electric guitar arpeggio,

programmed drum kit, a bright synth lead doubling the vocal melody, and
layered "oohs" as a backing hook. The bridge strips back to just piano and
vocal with a slow swell of strings entering underneath, before the full band
crashes back in for the final chorus with an added tambourine and a slightly
higher key-change feel in the melody line (same key, brighter voicing). The
outro fades the band out gradually, leaving the guitar arpeggio from verse
one alone at the very end, closing the loop.
```
